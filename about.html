The story

Bringing you a little taste of New Orleans.

Hook & Kee Creations is a Sanford bakery known for custom cakes, allergy-conscious desserts, and the OG ooey-gooey bars locals hunt down at the farmers market. Owners Akeashia and James build celebration cakes and specialty sweets with a personal touch — not mass production.
